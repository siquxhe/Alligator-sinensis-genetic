## 一、英文投稿版（canonical）

**Table 1. Viral fraction of reads under six reporting definitions in six Chinese alligator gut metagenomes.**

| Code | Definition | Denominator | Reference space | Assignment | W1 | W2 | W3 | S1 | S2 | S3 | Range (%) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **C1** | Strict: structural + replication/transcription categories only | Clean reads | RVDB-prot v32 Unique | Category filter | 0.1023 | 0.0891 | 0.0934 | 0.0962 | 0.1141 | 0.1220 | 0.0891–0.1220 |
| **C2** | Broad: all viral-like hits | Clean reads | RVDB-prot v32 Unique | No filter | 0.701 | 0.675 | 0.692 | 0.702 | 0.696 | 0.689 | 0.675–0.702 |
| **C6** | Control: broad, host reads retained | All QC reads (before host removal) | RVDB-prot v32 Unique | No filter | 0.6923 | 0.6744 | 0.6901 | 0.7019 | 0.6961 | 0.6892 | 0.6744–0.7019 |
| **C3** | Literature-reported value | — | nr + MEGAN4 (LCA) | LCA assignment | n.a. | n.a. | n.a. | n.a. | n.a. | n.a. | 0.04–4.03 (reported) |
| **C4** | Viral contigs / all contigs | All contigs | geNomad | Contig count | 1.87 | 1.41 | 4.92 | 2.63 | 13.34 | 12.70 | 1.41–13.34 |
| **C5** | vOTU-mapped reads | Clean reads | Study vOTUs (n = 9,980) | Read count | 2.62 | 3.72 | 3.91 | 2.14 | 12.81 | 15.23 | 2.14–15.23 |

**Notes.**

(a) Values are percentages: of reads for C1, C2, C5 and C6, and of contigs for C4. Samples are six fecal shotgun metagenomes of *Alligator sinensis* (PRJNA539906; Tang et al., *Front Microbiol* 2019, doi:10.3389/fmicb.2019.02409).

(b) Clean reads are reads after host removal, i.e. the input to viral screening. C6 uses the pre-depletion QC read count (host_removal_summary.total_reads × 2) and shares its numerator with C2; the two rows therefore differ only in the denominator, by ≤1.2%.

(c) C3 gives the range reported by the original study (0.04% during hibernation to 4.03% during the active phase; their Supplementary Table S19). That study does not specify how viral sequences were defined beyond referring to that table, and the value could not be recomputed here because the original ORF alignments were not retained. C3 is shown for orientation only and is excluded from every fold-change calculation.

(d) Viral-like hits were identified with DIAMOND blastx against RVDB-prot v32 Unique (alignment length ≥30 aa, identity ≥40%, E ≤ 1e-5). C1 retains only hits annotated to structural or replication/transcription categories; C2 applies no category filter.

(e) Contigs were assembled with MEGAHIT and classified with geNomad; vOTUs were clustered with CD-HIT-EST at 95% identity and 85% coverage of the shorter sequence (n = 9,980).

(f) Values are given at the precision produced by the analysis pipeline; fold changes between definitions are shown in Fig. 2B and are not multiplicative.

## 三、三个需要解释的编排决定

**1. 为什么 C3 留在表里，尽管全列 n.a.。**
它是全文唯一的外部锚点——读者需要知道"我们算出的 0.09–15% 与原文报的 0.04–4.03% 差多少"。删掉它，Table 1 就只剩自说自话；保留它并明确标 `n.a.` + 表注 (c)，既提供参照，又声明不可比。

**2. 为什么 C6 只有一行、且紧跟在 C2 后面。**
C6 与 C2 同分子、只换分母，放在一起读者能直接看出两行几乎重合——差异 ≤1.2%。这比在正文里写十句话都有说服力：**分母这一维在本批数据里确实不重要，但我们算了。**

**3. 为什么精度不统一（C1 四位、C2 三位、C4 两位）。**
按分析流程实际产出的精度呈现，不人为补齐。C2 的三位来自源表的 `viral_like_pct`；若强行写成 0.7010 会制造假精度。表注 (f) 已说明。
